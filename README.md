### A project I recommend

[SanSaSane/krita-mcp](https://github.com/SanSaSane/krita-mcp)

krita-mcp connects the drawing tool Krita to AI.

It is also great for people who draw by hand.  
It can look at your canvas and give you feedback on the layout and colors.

If you draw alone and feel stuck, it can be a real help.
